THE QUICK ANSWER
What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping finds. A useful entry includes a product link, category, listed price, size information, material notes and the date checked. Treat each entry as a starting point for verification, rather than proof of quality, authenticity or availability.

The research routine

Less scrolling.
More useful information.

Turn an Oopbuy product list into a shortlist you can actually compare with this three-step process.

STEP 01

Choose a purpose

Start with one category and a clear need. Decide on the fit, color, material and spending limit before collecting more links.

STEP 02

Capture the evidence

Save the exact listing, selected variant, measurements and date checked. Separate seller claims from details you can verify.

STEP 03

Compare the full picture

Review the item price alongside delivery, service charges and return terms. Recheck the listing before making a purchase.

An Oopbuy shopping guide

Read the details.
Then decide.

For an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet

Group tops, outerwear and bottoms separately. A hoodie needs chest and body measurements; pants need waist, rise, inseam and leg opening. Record whether the measurements describe the garment or the wearer.

For an Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet

Keep the listed size system beside the seller’s size chart. Check the model, upper construction and available fit information. If a measurement is missing or unclear, leave a question instead of inventing a value.

For Oopbuy finds across accessories

For jewelry, headwear and other accessories, dimensions and materials are especially useful. An attractive photo alone does not confirm weight, finish, comfort or authenticity.

Before you save a product

  • Make sure the link opens the intended product and variant.
  • Check that the description and images refer to the same item.
  • Compare measurements with an item you already own.
  • Record the currency and when the price was checked.
  • Review seller information and available return terms.
  • Confirm any shipping or service charges separately.
  • Mark missing details as unknown until verified.
  • Remove duplicates and revisit old entries before ordering.

Straight answers

A few things
to know first.

Clear distinctions make a shopping guide more useful. Here is what this page provides and what you should confirm elsewhere.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping finds. Useful fields include the product link, category, listed price, size information, material notes and the date the listing was checked.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS browsing and research guide. It does not represent Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page is a static browsing guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet. Its category links open CAFANS pages where you can continue product research.

Does a spreadsheet listing prove a product is authentic?

No. A spreadsheet entry or product image does not establish authenticity. Check the seller, supporting documentation, product details and return terms independently.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. For tops, check chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and body length. Do not rely only on a letter size.

Does the listed product price include every cost?

Do not assume it does. Confirm domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties before ordering.
